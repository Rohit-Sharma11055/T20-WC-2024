# T20 World Cup 2025 - Team India Tribute Website 🏆

A celebratory website built to honor **Team India’s glorious victory** in the **T20 World Cup 2024**. This project showcases the spirit of the nation and the incredible efforts of the players who made this championship unforgettable.

## 🎉 About the Project

This website is a digital tribute to the Indian cricket team, capturing:
- The **victory celebration**
- Profiles of the **players**
- A special **team photo with the trophy**

The design reflects national pride, team spirit, and the emotional journey of India's win.

## 🌟 Features

- 🏆 **Home Page** with celebration message and visuals
- 👕 **Player Profiles** with images, roles, and match highlights
- 📷 **Team Image** holding the T20 World Cup trophy
- 🇮🇳 Themed design to reflect the Indian tricolor and cricket passion

- **HTML**
- **CSS**
