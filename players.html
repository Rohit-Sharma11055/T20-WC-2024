PLAYERS

Rohit Sharma, a dynamic batsman aged 37, is renowned for his elegant 
stroke play and explosive performance in both Test and T20 formats. 
His exceptional ability to anchor innings and accelerate when needed 
makes him a crucial asset to Team India. Sharma’s skill and charisma 
continue to captivate cricket fans worldwide.
 
Virat Kohli, a 35-year-old legendary batsman, is celebrated for his 
aggressive style and impeccable technique. His consistency and ability 
to chase down targets have made him a cornerstone of Team India’s 
batting lineup. Kohli’s relentless drive and exceptional skill have 
earned him admiration as one of cricket's finest.
 
Yashasvi Jaiswal, a talented 22-year-old batsman, has quickly made a 
name for himself with his aggressive stroke play and solid technique. 
Known for his impressive performances and promising future, Jaiswal’s 
flair and dedication are poised to make significant contributions to 
Team India’s success in international cricket.
 
Suryakumar Yadav, a 33-year-old dynamic batsman, is renowned for 
his innovative stroke play and aggressive batting style. His ability 
to excel in T20s with unorthodox shots and consistent performances 
makes him a key player for Team India. Yadav's flair and versatility 
continue to dazzle cricket enthusiasts worldwide.
 
Rishabh Pant, a 26-year-old explosive batsman and wicketkeeper, is 
known for his aggressive approach and match-winning abilities. His 
fearless stroke play and sharp glovework have made him a standout
performer for Team India. Pant’s energy and talent are pivotal in
shaping the future of Indian cricket.
 
Sanju Samson, a 29-year-old stylish batsman, is celebrated for his 
elegant stroke play and impressive wicketkeeping skills. His ability 
to anchor innings and accelerate when needed makes him a valuable asset 
for Team India. Samson’s flair and consistency continue to enhance his 
reputation in international cricket.
 
Hardik Pandya, a 30-year-old all-rounder, excels as both a powerful
batsman and a effective bowler. His explosive hitting and crucial 
breakthroughs make him a key player for Team India. Pandya’s 
versatility and dynamic presence on the field are vital to the team’s 
success and strategy.
 
Shivam Dube, a 30-year-old all-rounder, shines as both a hard-hitting 
batsman and a useful bowler. Known for his power-hitting and ability 
to provide crucial breakthroughs, Dube’s versatility and dynamic 
performances add significant depth to Team India’s lineup. His all-
around skills are a valuable asset.
 
Ravindra Jadeja, a 35-year-old all-rounder, excels with his spin 
bowling, solid batting, and exceptional fielding. Renowned for his 
accuracy and versatility, Jadeja consistently delivers crucial 
performances across formats. His invaluable contributions with both 
bat and ball make him a cornerstone of Team India’s success.
 
Axar Patel, a 30-year-old left-arm spinner, excels in both bowling and

him a valuable asset for Team India. His reliable performances

Kuldeep Yadav, a 29-year-old left-arm wrist-spinner, is celebrated for

Yuzvendra Chahal, a 33-year-old leg-spinner, is renowned for his

Arshdeep Singh, a 25-year-old left-arm fast bowler, is known for his

Jasprit Bumrah, a 30-year-old premier fast bowler, is celebrated for
